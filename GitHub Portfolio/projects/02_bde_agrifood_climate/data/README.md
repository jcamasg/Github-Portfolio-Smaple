# Data

All series are generated locally and are explicitly labelled synthetic.

The data-generating process is designed to reproduce plausible research
features:

- persistent commodity-price levels;
- gradual price adjustment across value-chain stages;
- pass-through from energy and wage costs;
- different short-run responses to increases and decreases;
- product-specific exposure to ENSO; and
- climate-sensitive food output.

The repository does not include or claim to reproduce FAO, NOAA, Eurostat or
Banco de España datasets. Source names such as `synthetic_FAO_style` describe
the conceptual role of a table, not its provenance.
